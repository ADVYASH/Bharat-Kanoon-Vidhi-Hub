CHAPTER I

Preliminary

Sections 1–5

Introduces the short title, territorial extent, commencement, definitions and general rules concerning the application of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973.

CHAPTER II

Constitution of Criminal Courts and Offices

Sections 6–25A

Covers classes of criminal courts, territorial divisions, Courts of Session, judicial and executive magistrates, public prosecutors and prosecution directorates.

CHAPTER III

Power of Courts

Sections 26–35

Explains the courts competent to try offences, sentencing powers, limits on punishments and the conferral or exercise of judicial powers.

CHAPTER IV

Powers of Superior Officers of Police and Aid to the Magistrates and the Police

Sections 36–40

Addresses powers of superior police officers, public assistance to magistrates and police, reporting certain offences and related duties.

CHAPTER V

Arrest of Persons

Sections 41–60A

Covers arrest without warrant, arrest procedures, rights and safeguards, search of arrested persons, medical examination, production before magistrates and prevention of unnecessary restraint.

CHAPTER VI

Processes to Compel Appearance

Sections 61–90

Deals with summonses, warrants of arrest, proclamation for absconding persons, attachment of property and related procedures to secure appearance before a criminal court.

CHAPTER VII

Processes to Compel the Production of Things

Sections 91–105

Covers production of documents and things, search warrants, searches, seizure of property and the procedures governing these processes.

CHAPTER VIIA

Reciprocal Arrangements for Assistance in Certain Matters and Procedure for Attachment and Forfeiture of Property

Sections 105A–105L

Addresses international assistance in criminal matters, transfer of persons, investigation-related cooperation and procedures for attachment or forfeiture of property in specified cases.

CHAPTER VIII

Security for Keeping the Peace and for Good Behaviour

Sections 106–124

Covers security following conviction, proceedings to maintain peace, good-behaviour bonds, inquiries and orders in preventive proceedings.

CHAPTER IX

Order for Maintenance of Wives, Children and Parents

Sections 125–128

Provides procedures for maintenance claims by eligible wives, children and parents, including enforcement of maintenance orders.

CHAPTER X

Maintenance of Public Order and Tranquillity

Sections 129–148

Addresses dispersal of unlawful assemblies, public nuisances, urgent orders, land and water disputes likely to cause breaches of peace and other public-order matters.

CHAPTER XI

Preventive Action of the Police

Sections 149–153

Covers police duties to prevent cognizable offences, communicate information about offences and take specified preventive measures.

CHAPTER XII

Information to the Police and Their Powers to Investigate

Sections 154–176

Covers information in cognizable cases, FIR registration, investigation, police statements, searches, case diaries, final reports and inquiries into suspicious deaths.

CHAPTER XIII

Jurisdiction of Criminal Courts in Inquiries and Trials

Sections 177–189

Explains territorial jurisdiction, place of inquiry and trial, offences committed across locations and rules for determining the appropriate court.

CHAPTER XIV

Conditions Requisite for Initiation of Proceedings

Sections 190–199

Covers cognizance of offences by magistrates, transfer of cases, statutory complaints, sanctions for prosecution and restrictions on initiating specified criminal proceedings.

CHAPTER XV

Complaints to Magistrates

Sections 200–210

Addresses examination of complainants and witnesses, postponement of process, dismissal of complaints and procedures following the taking of cognizance.

CHAPTER XVI

Commencement of Proceedings Before Magistrates

Sections 211–224

Covers issue of process, supply of documents, commitment of cases to the Court of Session and commencement of proceedings before magistrates.

CHAPTER XVII

The Charge

Sections 225–237

Explains the form and contents of charges, particulars of offences, alteration of charges, joinder of charges and related rules.

CHAPTER XVIII

Trial Before a Court of Session

Sections 238–250

Covers discharge, framing of charges, prosecution evidence, defence, arguments, judgment and related procedures in Sessions trials.

CHAPTER XIX

Trial of Warrant-Cases by Magistrates

Sections 251–259

Addresses warrant-case trials instituted on police reports or otherwise, including discharge, charges, evidence and acquittal or conviction.

CHAPTER XX

Trial of Summons-Cases by Magistrates

Sections 260–265

Covers the procedure for summons-case trials, substance of accusations, pleas, conviction, acquittal and related trial proceedings.

CHAPTER XXI

Summary Trials

Sections 266–271

Provides simplified trial procedures for eligible offences, including authority to conduct summary trials and the required record of proceedings.

CHAPTER XXIA

Plea Bargaining

Sections 265A–265L

Covers eligibility, applications, mutually satisfactory dispositions, court procedures and judgments in qualifying plea-bargaining cases.

CHAPTER XXII

Attendance of Persons Confined or Detained in Prisons

Sections 266–271

Addresses production of prisoners, attendance of detained persons, warrants, exemptions and related procedures for criminal proceedings.

CHAPTER XXIII

Evidence in Inquiries and Trials

Sections 272–299

Covers recording of evidence, witness attendance, commissions for examination, expert evidence, affidavits and evidentiary procedures in inquiries and criminal trials.

CHAPTER XXIV

General Provisions as to Inquiries and Trials

Sections 300–327

Contains general rules concerning previous convictions or acquittals, legal representation, adjournments, compounding of offences, examination of accused persons and related trial matters.

CHAPTER XXV

Provisions as to Accused Persons of Unsound Mind

Sections 328–339

Addresses inquiries into mental condition, fitness to stand trial, treatment and custody arrangements, resumption of proceedings and related judicial safeguards.

CHAPTER XXVI

Provisions as to Offences Affecting the Administration of Justice

Sections 340–352

Covers complaints concerning specified offences against public justice, perjury-related procedures, contempt-related matters and procedures for prosecution in defined circumstances.

CHAPTER XXVII

The Judgment

Sections 353–365

Covers pronouncement and contents of judgments, compensation, probation-related matters, victim-related orders and communication of judicial outcomes.

CHAPTER XXVIII

Submission of Death Sentences for Confirmation

Sections 366–371

Sets out the procedure for submitting death sentences to the High Court for confirmation, consideration of records and related appellate powers and decisions.

CHAPTER XXIX

Appeals

Sections 372–394

Covers appeal rights, appeal procedures, suspension of sentences, appellate hearings, powers of appellate courts and finality of specified appellate decisions.

CHAPTER XXX

Reference and Revision

Sections 395–405

Addresses references to the High Court and revisional powers of higher courts, including examination of records and correction of specified procedural or judicial errors.

CHAPTER XXXI

Transfer of Criminal Cases

Sections 406–412

Covers the powers and procedures for transferring criminal cases and appeals between courts in specified circumstances.

CHAPTER XXXII

Execution, Suspension, Remission and Commutation of Sentences

Sections 413–435

Addresses execution of sentences, imprisonment, warrants, suspension, remission, commutation, and the powers of appropriate authorities concerning sentences.

CHAPTER XXXIII

Provisions as to Bail and Bonds

Sections 436–450

Covers bail in bailable and non-bailable cases, anticipatory bail, bonds, sureties, cancellation of bail and related procedures for release from custody.

CHAPTER XXXIV

Disposal of Property

Sections 451–459

Addresses interim custody, disposal, restoration and handling of property produced before courts or connected with criminal proceedings.

CHAPTER XXXV

Irregular Proceedings

Sections 460–466

Explains specified procedural irregularities, their legal consequences and circumstances in which proceedings may or may not be invalidated.

CHAPTER XXXVI

Limitation for Taking Cognizance of Certain Offences

Sections 467–473

Covers limitation periods for taking cognizance of certain offences, computation and exclusion of time, extension of limitation and related rules.

CHAPTER XXXVII

Miscellaneous

Sections 474–484

Contains miscellaneous procedural provisions, including rule-making powers, forms, inherent powers of the High Court, publication of proceedings and repeal and savings.
