Covers conditions, sanctions and restrictions applicable to cognizance and initiation of specified criminal proceedings.

Sections 223–226

Deals with examination of complainants, complaint procedures, postponement of process and dismissal of complaints.

CHAPTER XVII

Commencement of Proceedings Before Magistrates

Sections 227–233

Explains issuance of process, supply of documents and commencement of proceedings before magistrates.

Attendance of Persons Confined or Detained in Prisons

Sections 301–306

Deals with legal procedures for securing attendance of prisoners or detained persons for criminal proceedings.
