# ⚖️ Bharat Kanoon Vidhi Hub

**Bharat Kanoon Vidhi Hub** is a legal knowledge platform dedicated to making Indian laws, Bare Acts, court judgments, landmark cases, legal documents, and judicial information accessible to students, advocates, researchers, and citizens.

## 🌐 About the Project
The platform aims to promote legal awareness, simplify legal learning, and provide organized resources related to the Indian legal system.

## ✨ Features
- Indian laws and Bare Acts
- Court judgments and landmark cases
- Legal documents and educational resources
- Criminal and civil law information
- Legal news and judicial updates
- User-friendly interface for legal research

## 🎯 Mission
To make legal knowledge accessible to everyone and encourage awareness of constitutional values, legal rights, duties, and justice.

## 👥 Target Audience
- Law students
- Advocates and legal professionals
- Legal researchers
- Citizens interested in Indian law

## ⚠️ Disclaimer
This project is intended for educational and informational purposes only. It does not constitute professional legal advice. Always verify legal information through authoritative sources.
