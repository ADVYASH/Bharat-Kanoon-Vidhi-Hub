CHAPTER I

Introduction

Sections 1–5

Introduces the title, territorial extent, operation, definitions and rules concerning the application of the Indian Penal Code, 1860.

CHAPTER II

General Explanations

Sections 6–52A

Explains legal expressions, public servants, wrongful gain and loss, dishonesty, fraud, voluntary conduct, joint liability and other general principles used throughout the Code.

CHAPTER III

Of Punishments

Sections 53–75

Covers punishments under the Code, including imprisonment, fine, commutation, solitary confinement and enhanced punishment for certain repeat offences.

CHAPTER IV

General Exceptions

Sections 76–106

Addresses mistake of fact, judicial acts, accidents, necessity, infancy, unsoundness of mind, consent, compulsion and the right of private defence.

CHAPTER V

Of Abetment

Sections 107–120

Explains abetment, abettors, liability for abetted offences, punishment for abetment and related rules concerning criminal participation.

CHAPTER VA

Criminal Conspiracy

Sections 120A–120B

Defines criminal conspiracy and provides punishment for agreements to commit illegal acts or acts made illegal through specified means.

CHAPTER VI

Of Offences Against the State

Sections 121–130

Covers waging war against the Government of India, conspiracy, sedition under the historical Code, assistance to State prisoners and related offences. Individual provisions must be checked against applicable law.

CHAPTER VII

Of Offences Relating to the Army, Navy and Air Force

Sections 131–140

Addresses abetment of mutiny, desertion, harbouring deserters, military insubordination and other specified offences relating to the armed forces.

CHAPTER VIII

Of Offences Against the Public Tranquillity

Sections 141–160

Covers unlawful assemblies, rioting, common-object liability, affray and other offences involving public disorder and breaches of the peace.

CHAPTER IX

Of Offences by or Relating to Public Servants

Sections 161–171

Contains historical provisions concerning bribery, corruption-related conduct and offences involving public servants. Several provisions have been repealed or affected by later legislation.

CHAPTER IXA

Of Offences Relating to Elections

Sections 171A–171I

Covers election-related definitions and offences involving bribery, undue influence, personation, false statements and unlawful election payments.

CHAPTER X

Of Contempts of the Lawful Authority of Public Servants

Sections 172–190

Addresses evasion of legal process, failure to attend when required, obstruction of public servants, disobedience of lawful orders and related offences.

CHAPTER XI

Of False Evidence and Offences Against Public Justice

Sections 191–229A

Covers false evidence, fabrication of evidence, false charges, harbouring offenders, escape from custody, obstruction of justice and related offences.

CHAPTER XII

Of Offences Relating to Coin and Government Stamps

Sections 230–263A

Addresses counterfeiting and specified offences involving coins, Government stamps, currency-related instruments and associated materials.

CHAPTER XIII

Of Offences Relating to Weights and Measures

Sections 264–267

Covers specified offences involving fraudulent use of false weighing instruments, false weights and false measures in commercial or other transactions.

CHAPTER XIV

Of Offences Affecting Public Health, Safety, Convenience, Decency and Morals

Sections 268–294A

Covers public nuisance, negligent acts, dangerous conduct, adulteration, spread of disease, obscenity, public health and safety and specified public-order offences.

CHAPTER XV

Of Offences Relating to Religion

Sections 295–298

Addresses specified offences involving places of worship, religious feelings, deliberate acts concerning religious beliefs and disturbance of religious assemblies.

CHAPTER XVI

Of Offences Affecting the Human Body

Sections 299–377

Covers culpable homicide, murder, hurt, wrongful restraint, wrongful confinement, assault, kidnapping, abduction, sexual offences and other offences affecting the human body.

CHAPTER XVII

Of Offences Against Property

Sections 378–462

Addresses theft, extortion, robbery, dacoity, criminal misappropriation, criminal breach of trust, cheating, mischief, criminal trespass and related offences against property.

CHAPTER XVIII

Of Offences Relating to Documents and Property Marks

Sections 463–489E

Covers forgery, false documents, forged records, falsification of accounts, counterfeit currency-related instruments and offences involving property marks.

CHAPTER XIX

Of the Criminal Breach of Contracts of Service

Sections 490–492

Contains historical provisions relating to specified breaches of service contracts. These provisions should be checked for repeal and current applicability.

CHAPTER XX

Of Offences Relating to Marriage

Sections 493–498

Addresses specified offences relating to marriage, deception concerning lawful marriage, bigamy, fraudulent marriage ceremonies and related conduct.

CHAPTER XXA

Of Cruelty by Husband or Relatives of Husband

Section 498A

Provides the historical IPC provision concerning cruelty by a husband or his relatives towards a woman, subject to applicable law and transitional provisions.

CHAPTER XXI

Of Defamation

Sections 499–502

Defines criminal defamation, specifies exceptions and addresses punishment and publication or sale of defamatory material.

CHAPTER XXII

Of Criminal Intimidation, Insult and Annoyance

Sections 503–510

Covers criminal intimidation, intentional insult, statements conducing to public mischief, anonymous communications, misconduct in specified circumstances and related offences.

CHAPTER XXIII

Of Attempts to Commit Offences

Section 511

Provides the general historical IPC provision concerning attempts to commit offences punishable with imprisonment for life or other imprisonment, where the Code does not separately provide for the attempt.
